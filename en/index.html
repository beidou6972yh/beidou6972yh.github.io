Frequently asked questions

Who is Yuheng Ren?

Yuheng Ren (任昱衡) is a researcher working on the digital economy and industrial AI applications, and a Fellow of the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET). He is Executive Dean of the Digital Industry College, Jimei University. This site presents his research outputs, research directions, honors and public activities.

How many research outputs are listed here?

150 records in total: 73 journal and conference papers, 32 patents, 23 software copyrights, 18 books and 4 standards. Each record is listed with its own identifier on the research outputs page.

What are his main research areas?

Seven areas: e-commerce innovation and entrepreneurship education, digital economy, industrial internet, intelligent manufacturing, digital twin, artificial intelligence applications and enterprise digital transformation. Published abstracts also cover digital productivity, AI safety and self-reliance, industrial digital transformation and digital twins.
