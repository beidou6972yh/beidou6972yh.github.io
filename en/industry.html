Overview

Directions, content and role

The work covers e-commerce and e-government for state-owned enterprises, intelligent manufacturing, industrial digital twins, smart home, the industrial internet and digital-industry college development, spanning planning studies, platform construction, technical systems and project delivery. His roles include principal investigator, on-site project lead, head of the master-planning group, lead investigator, chairman of the project management committee and executive dean.
