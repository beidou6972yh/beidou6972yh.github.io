Overview

Directions, content and role

Research centres on the digital economy and digital productivity, AI safety and self-reliance, industrial digital transformation, digital twins and digital-industry talent development, covering publicly funded research, major consultancy and teaching-reform projects. His roles are principal investigator, project participant and industry–university collaborative researcher.
